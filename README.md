ManeuverNodeTelemetry , highly customizable flight telemetry HUD with a quick-access toolbar for Ksp2 Redux.

ManeuverNodeTelemetry gives you direct, real-time access to vital orbital , node maneuvers, surface dynamics, delta-V metrics, and target tracking information.

Key Features:

Quick-Bar: A draggable, floating dock featuring pixel-art icons that allow one-click toggling between dedicated telemetry pages and mod settings.
On-Page Drag-and-Drop Reordering: Easily rearrange telemetry rows directly on the active HUD window. Simply click and drag any metric up or down.
Persistent Auto-Saving: All custom metric selections, row ordering, window opacity, font sizes, icon scaling, button bindings, and color profiles are automatically saved to config.json and remembered between game sessions.
Full Appearance Customization: HSV Color Mixer: adjust Hue, Saturation, and Brightness independently for Accent borders, Labels, Values, and Icons. Scalable typography & icons and fine-tune background transparency.
Hotkeys: Press F8 to instantly show or hide the active telemetry window.

Installation:
Download the latest ManeuverNodeTelemetry-x.x.x.zip from the Releases page.
Extract the contents directly into your KSP2 mod folder.

Controls & Tips:
Left Click & Drag Header or the bottom of the window: Move the primary HUD window or settings window across your screen.
Left Click & Drag Bar Grip (||): Reposition the quick-bar anywhere on your screen.
Bottom-Right Grip (◲): Resize windows to fit your layout.
Drag Grip (::): Click and drag individual metric rows up or down on any page to reorder them.
